Meet Leanne

Your guide through the wild terrain of birth

Leanne is a qualified Hypnobirthing Practitioner, Doula and Yoga teacher based in Leicester — supporting birthing people across the Midlands and online.

As featured on BBC Radio Leicester, her approach is warm, evidence-based and unapologetically alternative. She believes every person deserves to feel informed, empowered and held on their birth journey.
