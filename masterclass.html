The birth masterclass that changes everything

This free, 60-minute online masterclass is Leanne's gift to every pregnant person. No cost, no catch — just a seriously comprehensive introduction to everything that can help you have a more positive birth.

What's covered

  • How your body is designed to give birth — and why understanding this changes everything
  • The fear-tension-pain cycle and how to break it
  • The key breathing techniques for labour
  • How to create a birth plan that actually works
  • Your rights during labour and birth
  • What to expect in early, active and transition labour
  • How your birth partner can genuinely help
  • Q&A — Leanne answers the questions nobody else does

Suitable for any stage of pregnancy. No prior knowledge needed.
