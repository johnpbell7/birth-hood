## The "my choice" lane

The strongest seller — widest market, and the line people photograph.

- **MY BIRTH. MY BODY. MY FUCKING CHOICE.** — the hero
- MY BIRTH. MY RULES.
- IT'S MY BIRTH AND I'LL DECLINE IF I WANT TO
- NOT YOUR CALL.
- ASK ME. DON'T TELL ME.
- MY BODY. MY BABY. MY DECISION. — clean sister design

## Before printing

**Paid ads.** Meta and TikTok reject uncensored profanity, and some
print-on-demand suppliers won't run it. Print `MY F*CKING CHOICE` as a second
colourway so there's a version that can be boosted. Same reasoning for keeping
a clean "MY BODY. MY BABY. MY DECISION." for gifting.

**Prior use.** "No is a complete sentence" and "Birth without the bullshit" are
both in circulation in this space. Run an IPO trade mark search and a plain
Google before adopting either as a brand line rather than a one-off design.
