About Leanne

A birth keeper who actually gets it

Hi, I'm Leanne — a qualified Hypnobirthing Practitioner, Doula and Prenatal Yoga Teacher based in Leicester. I've been supporting birthing people across the Midlands and online for over six years, and I've witnessed first-hand just how transformative the right support can be.

My journey into birth work started with my own birth experiences. I felt that something was missing — a genuine, honest space where pregnant people could ask real questions, receive grounded information and feel truly seen. That's what birth-hood was born from.

I support ALL pregnancies, ALL modes of birth and ALL people. I am fiercely non-judgmental. Whether you're planning a natural home birth, a caesarean, or are simply taking it one day at a time — you're welcome here.

I was featured on BBC Radio Leicester and continue to be a vocal advocate for informed, empowered birth choices in the local community and beyond.
