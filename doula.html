What is a Doula?

Your dedicated birth companion

A doula is a trained birth professional who provides continuous, non-medical support throughout your pregnancy, birth and postnatal period. Unlike midwives and doctors, a doula's sole focus is your emotional, physical and informational wellbeing.

Research shows that having continuous support during labour can lead to shorter labours, reduced need for pain relief, lower rates of caesarean section and higher satisfaction with the birth experience.

Leanne supports all families — same-sex couples, single parents, first-time parents, those who've had previous traumatic experiences, and everyone in between.

The evidence speaks for itself

25%
Reduction in caesarean births with continuous support
31%
Less likely to use pain medication
34%
Less likely to rate their birth experience negatively

Source: Cochrane Review, Continuous Support for Women During Childbirth
